## 0. Thesis

> **Anyone can run an export or import company with zero to two people. Zimplifyed supplies the rest of the company as AI agent employees, and sells the result.**

Three shifts over three years:

| | Today (2026) | Year 3 (2029) |
|---|---|---|
| What the customer buys | Software seats + AI actions | **Results under SLA** (a shipment documented, an invoice collected, a buyer qualified), plus the platform |
| Who does the work | The customer, assisted by AI drafts | **Agent employees**, with the customer approving exceptions and a licensed human backstop for licensed acts |
| Who the customer is | Existing exporters who run on WhatsApp, Tally and Excel | Those **plus Day-0 traders** who have never exported and **importers** |

**North star, in two stages:**
1. **Now → Year 2:** *zero-touch order rate*, unchanged as ratified, including the public-claim embargo (≥10% sustained for 8 weeks) and the strict denominator.
2. **From Year 2:** *results delivered within SLA per month*, with zero-touch order rate kept as the quality guard beneath it. A result that needed a human edit still counts as delivered, but not as zero-touch. The definition will live in `specs/METRICS_DEFINITIONS.md` (§9).

**The minimal-headcount metric we optimise for customers:** *export revenue per human in the tenant*. The Year 3 showcase is a one-person tenant shipping **US$1M a year** with no other staff.

## 1. Planning against the AI trajectory

### 1.1 Assumptions as of September 2026 (the CAIO seat re-checks them monthly)
- **Task horizon.** Frontier agents reliably complete multi-hour tasks with tools today. The trend is toward multi-day, multi-system tasks with self-verification.
- **Cost.** The price of a given capability level keeps falling steeply, historically around 5–10× a year. What a frontier model costs today, a small model will do cheaply within 12–24 months.
- **Modalities.** Voice (including Indian languages), document vision and computer use are becoming native. That matters because many exporter workflows live in PDFs, government portals and WhatsApp voice notes.
- **Interoperability.** Tool protocols (MCP) and agent-to-agent protocols are standardising. Buyers, banks, forwarders and governments will expose agent-callable surfaces.
- **The AGI question.** Credible forecasts range from "very capable, narrow agents" to "near-general reasoning by 2028–29". We do not bet on either; we build so both are good for us.

### 1.2 Three scenarios and what changes

| | **Slow** | **Base** | **Fast (near-AGI by 2028–29)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal | Accuracy on our golden doc sets plateaus below ~95% field-level; agent runs still need frequent help | Steady gains; L2 reached on core flows by 2027, L3 roles by 2028 | Models clear our evals with no prompt work; long-horizon agents handle whole shipments; government and bank APIs open up |
| Product response | Stay assist-heavy; lean on templates, rules and the human backstop; move fintech earlier (VISION_1B kill criterion) | This roadmap as written | Pull L3/L4 forward one year; shrink backstop share per result; expand results into higher-value work (negotiation, trade finance packaging) |
| Business response | Price agent-months and seats; RaaS limited to doc-sets and collections | Blend of per-result and subscription pricing | Price mostly per result or as % of value; guarantees become the product |
| Biggest risk | Burn on a workforce nobody trusts | Execution speed | **Commoditisation**: horizontal agents "do exports" without us |

### 1.3 What never changes: the moats that survive a smarter model

When intelligence is cheap, value moves to what a model cannot provide on its own:

1. **Accountability.** A named result, an SLA, evidence, and later an insurance-backed guarantee. Somebody has to be liable; a model cannot be.
2. **Licensed rails.** Relationships with licensed CHAs, CAs, GSPs, payment and FX partners, insurers. Acts that legally require a licence are a gate a model cannot pass.
3. **Integrations and system of record.** Orders, documents, payments and realization history in one place. Agents need somewhere to act from, and switching away is painful.
4. **Proprietary outcome data.** Accepted, edited and rejected outputs, customs and bank discrepancies, delivered results. That is our eval set and our underwriting data.
5. **Country and industry pack depth.** Provenance-tracked rules, verified by advisors, per corridor.
6. **The network.** Vendors, buyers, forwarders and inspectors connected through real transactions.

**Design rule that follows (CAIO + CTO).** Build the **harness**, not model-specific workarounds: evals per flow, the autonomy dial, permission-gated tools, budgets, audit, grounding. Then every model upgrade is a config change whose effect we *measure*. If a new model makes a workaround unnecessary, we delete the workaround. If a new model makes a role possible, the roster grows.

### 1.4 Triggers (reviewed monthly by the CAIO, decided by the CEO)
- A new model lifts approve-without-edit on a flow by ≥10 points in evals → re-run the promotion analysis for that flow across tenants.
- A government or bank portal ships a usable API → the CCO reviews licensed-act boundaries, and the COO decides whether a backstop step can become a partner API call.
- A buyer-side agent contacts ≥5 tenants in a month → pull forward the agent-to-agent surfaces (charter §9).
- Cost per result falls below 30% of price for two consecutive months → the CFO reviews price cuts or scope expansion.

## 3. The RaaS catalogue

### 3.1 What a "result" is
A result is a **sellable unit of finished work** with:
- **Definition of done.** Observable and evidence-backed, for example "doc set approved by owner, shared with CHA, zero open `problems[]`".
- **SLA.** Time from all inputs present to done.
- **Evidence.** The artefacts, audit trail and AgentRun log attached to the result record.
- **Backstop.** The named human role (the owner, or a partner from the COO network) that finishes it when the agent cannot.
- **Price and cost-to-serve**, owned by the CFO.

### 3.2 The catalogue (Year 1–3)

| # | Result | Delivered by (charter §7) | Licensed act → who performs | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Shipment documented**: complete, cross-validated doc set approved and shared | Documentation Officer | None (customs filing stays with the CHA) | 1 |
| R2 | **Invoice collected and realized**: paid, allocated, realization evidence closed | Finance Controller | Bank realization reporting → tenant or bank | 1 |
| R3 | **Quote sent and followed up**: every enquiry answered and chased through the ladder | Sales Executive | None | 1 (included in plans) |
| R4 | **Export-ready company**: registrations checklist completed and verified (IEC, GST + LUT, bank AD code, RCMC as applicable) | Export Manager + Compliance Officer | Registrations → tenant, assisted by partner CA | 1 (design partners) → 2 |
| R5 | **Qualified buyer shortlist**: N importers for an HS code and market, enriched and screened | Market & Buyer Research Analyst | None | 2 |
| R6 | **Order sourced within margin**: vendor confirmed, PO approved, cost above floor | Sourcing & Procurement Officer | None | 2 |
| R7 | **Shipment booked and tracked** | Logistics Coordinator | Customs broking → partner CHA | 2 |
| R8 | **Incentive claimed** | Incentives Officer | Claim filing → tenant or partner | 2 |
| R9 | **Import landed-cost decision + import cleared** | Import Desk Officer | Bill-of-entry filing → partner customs broker | 2 |
| R10 | **Pre-shipment inspection passed** | Production & QC Coordinator | Inspection → partner inspector | 2 |
| R11 | **Repeat order secured** | Buyer Success Officer | None | 3 |
| R12 | **Negotiated freight within band** (L4) | Logistics Coordinator | None | 3 |
| R13 | **First export shipment**: the Day-0 bundle (R4 → R5 → R6 → R1 → R7 → R2 for the first order) | Export Manager orchestrating all | As above | 3 |

### 3.3 Pricing ladder and unit economics (proposed, pending CFO and founder sign-off)

Pricing moves up a ladder as trust is earned. The ratified rungs are unchanged:

1. **Plans** (Free / Solo ₹999 / Starter ₹1,499 / Growth ₹4,999 / Enterprise), ratified.
2. **Agent-month** (₹2,999 for 500 actions, ₹4,999 for 1,500 actions, Growth+ required), ratified for Phase C.
3. **Per result** (new, **proposed**), with indicative anchors to be modelled by the CFO:
   - R1 shipment documented: ~₹499–999 per shipment. This is distinct from the existing ₹99/doc-set generation overage, which remains the self-serve tool price. R1 adds cross-validation to done, CHA share, SLA and backstop review.
   - R2 collected and realized: ~0.2–0.4% of invoice value with a floor and cap, **billed on our own invoice, never deducted from the tenant's proceeds** (we do not touch their money).
   - R4 export-ready: a one-time fee; partner CA fees billed by the partner directly.
   - R8 incentive claimed: a % of the incentive realized (VISION_1B anchor: ~3%).
4. **% of value / guarantee-backed** (Year 3, **proposed**): results carrying an insurance-backed guarantee are priced with a reserve built in.

**Economics guardrails (proposed).**
- Blended gross margin per result ≥60% after AI, backstop and partner cost. Software lines keep the ≥75% rule.
- Cost-to-serve is instrumented per result from the first design-partner result, before any price is set.
- A guarantee reserve of ~3–5% of guaranteed-result revenue from Year 3.
- A result SKU is withdrawn if it runs below floor GM for two consecutive quarters, unless the CEO logs it as a deliberate loss-leader.

## 5. Year 2 — Q4 2027 → Q3 2028: "Employ: the AI workforce goes GA"

**Maps to:** ROADMAP Phase C (2027 H2 → 2028 H1) and the start of Phase D.

**Goal:** tenants *hire* agent employees from a roster, buy results from a catalogue with SLAs, and a Day-0 trader can go from signup to export-ready without a consultant.

**Build:**
1. **L3 agent employees GA** (ROADMAP Phase C), expanding from the ratified Sales / Docs / Finance trio to **8+ roles**: add Sourcing, Production & QC, Logistics, Compliance and Incentives.
   - Roster UI covering hire, job description, autonomy dial per flow, activity feed and unhire.
   - The durable AgentRun step-log; per-agent budgets; agent-month pricing live, as ratified.
2. **RaaS catalogue GA** for R1–R10, with published SLAs and, where legality allows, per-result pricing (CFO + founder sign-off per SKU).
3. **Backstop partner network v1** (COO): onboarded, licence-verified CHAs, CAs, inspectors and forwarders, with routing from `ask_for_help` on a sold result, quality scoring per partner, and partners billing tenants directly.
4. **Day-0 bundle**: R4 export-ready plus R5 buyer shortlist, with guided first-quote and first-sample flows. Target: signup → export-ready in ≤30 days.
5. **Importer desk** (persona 7): landed-cost decision, supplier documents and clearance coordination via a partner broker. This is the India import pack.
6. **Manufacturer depth** per Phase C: production and QC, packing, inventory-lite, sampling, vendor scorecards, container tracking, certificate management.
7. **Money depth** per Phase C: bank reconciliation → Account Aggregator, FX exposure, and GST via GSP (legal review done in Phase B; filing stays L0).
8. **Corridors**: the UAE/GCC pack with advisor sign-off (master plan), plus one more corridor chosen by CCO and CEO against the pack contract.
9. **Fintech referrals attached to results** (Phase D start): collections/FX and cargo insurance offered at the moment R1 or R2 is delivered, through licensed partners only, at take rates, with no balance-sheet risk.
10. **Agent-to-agent readiness** (charter §9): signed agent identity and machine-readable quote, catalogue, track and doc-set endpoints behind tenant-issued tokens.

**Company side:** SOC 2 Type I when the first enterprise deal needs it (CISO). Hire the AI engineer (CAIO counterpart), the trade-domain expert (~150 paying orgs), AEs and a partner manager on PQL and partner triggers.

**Gates to Year 3:**
- Zero-touch order rate ≥25% (ROADMAP Phase C gate); agent-month or result attach ≥15% of paid orgs.
- Doc-set field-level accuracy ≥98% (VISION_1B kill criterion). If missed, stay assist-heavy and move to the slow scenario (§1.2).
- Results within SLA ≥95% across the catalogue; blended result GM ≥60%.

## 6. Year 3 — Q4 2028 → Q3 2029: "Deliver: the autonomous trade company"

**Maps to:** ROADMAP Phase D (2028) and the start of Phase E (2029+).

**Goal:** a one- or two-person company runs a multi-market export or import business on Zimplifyed, buying mostly results, with bounded autonomy on the high-volume decisions.

**Build:**
1. **L4 within policy bounds** (ROADMAP Phase E, pulled forward only if §1.4 triggers fire and the CCO clears each flow):
   - freight booking within a rate band with approved forwarders;
   - Sales concessions within an owner-set band;
   - reorders from buyer cadence;
   - dunning escalations.

   Filings and payments remain L0/L1.
2. **Guaranteed results**: insurance-backed guarantees on R1 (VISION_1B §3: "if a validated doc set is rejected for an inconsistency we missed, we cover the amendment cost"). Extend to R2 or R7 only once measured error rates support pricing.
3. **R13 First export shipment** as a flagship Day-0 product: zero to first realized shipment orchestrated by the Export Manager, with a success fee on realization.
4. **Agent-to-agent trade live**: buyer procurement agents query catalogues, request quotes, negotiate inside bands and pull doc sets. All of it is logged to the lead timeline and bounded by policy.
5. **Network**: the buyer workspace/portal (Phase D), the CHA collaboration workspace, the vendor graph with verified execution history, anonymised opt-in benchmarks, the public API, webhooks and Tally sync.
6. **Corridors 3–4** on the proven pack contract (Vietnam and Bangladesh were named in ROADMAP Phase D; final choice by CCO and CEO on demand).
7. **Fintech engine 3 at scale** (VISION_1B §4): cross-border collections and FX, trade-finance origination on execution data, and insurance, all via licensed partners.

**Company side:** the company still aims for fewer humans than a comparable SaaS. Hire counsel, a security engineer, a fintech partnerships lead and the first country GMs per `TEAMS_AND_ORG_PLAN.md`; each AI seat has, or is, a human's staff.

**Year 3 outcomes we would call success (base scenario):**
- Results within SLA are the majority of revenue growth, and per-result plus agent-month revenue exceeds seat revenue.
- A showcase cohort of one-person tenants each shipping ≥US$1M a year.
- Export revenue per human in the tenant is tracked and rising cohort over cohort.
- VISION_1B milestone trajectory: US$10M ARR territory, with engine 2 (AI work priced per outcome) live and engine 3 started.

## 8. Risks and kill criteria

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation | Kill or pivot signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Liability for results** | Selling a result implies responsibility when it is wrong | Definitions-of-done with evidence; disclaimers on compliance; licensed acts with licensed parties; guarantees only once priced from measured error rates | Guarantee claims >5% of guaranteed-result revenue for two quarters → suspend guarantees |
| **Licensed-activity creep** | An agent "helpfully" doing customs broking, filing or payments | Ceilings in charter §7 and standing rules §8; CCO legality review per SKU; tools that do not exist cannot be called | Any breach is a P0 incident; that flow freezes to L1 |
| **Horizontal agents commoditise workflow** | Frontier labs or big platforms ship "export agents" | Moats in §1.3: accountability, rails, system of record, outcome data, packs, network | Win rate vs horizontal tools drops below 50% in lost-deal reviews → accelerate fintech and network |
| **Model/vendor dependency** | Price hikes, outages, policy changes | Multi-provider router with fallback; every flow eval-gated so a switch is measured | A single provider's outage causes a missed-SLA day → fallback provider becomes mandatory per flow |
| **Trust incident** | One bad autonomous send damages a tenant's buyer relationship | Single-shot gate, four kill switches, auto-demotion, agent activity feed | Any L3/L4 incident with external harm → that flow demotes across all tenants pending review |
| **Prompt injection via inbound** | Buyers, spammers or attackers email instructions to agents | Inbound is data (standing rule 4); tool scopes; no credentials in agents | Detected injection that reached a tool call → CISO freeze and review |
| **Backstop quality** | Partners are the weakest link in an SLA | Licence verification, per-partner scoring, redundancy per region | Partner-caused SLA misses >20% of misses → rebuild routing and partner mix |
| **Day-0 traders churn before first shipment** | Longest time-to-value of any persona | R4 → R5 → first sample flow; the Export Manager chases; partner CA assist | <20% of Day-0 signups export-ready within 60 days → rethink the bundle before R13 |
| **Regulatory change (DPDP, e-invoicing, trade policy)** | Packs go stale; data rules tighten | CCO regulatory-change lead-time KPI; Mumbai residency at ~1k users (ratified) | — |
| **Execution capacity (ours)** | Founder + Claude Code is the bottleneck | Loop-first order; RaaS layered on existing primitives, not new surfaces; hires on triggers | A wave slips >2× estimate twice → the CEO cuts scope, never the gates |
